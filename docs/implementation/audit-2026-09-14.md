# Historical architecture/code audit — 2026-09-14

This multi-agent audit is source evidence for the later refactoring effort, not the current architecture or task
queue. Its useful themes—credential/deployment-coupled reads, SQL-heavy business logic, duplicate implementations,
ignored CI, source-shape gates and accumulating generations—were rechecked against newer source and distilled into
[Issue #380](https://github.com/UnknownAlienHuman/eliot-research/issues/380), the
[product manifesto](../architecture/ELIOT_RESEARCH.md), [implementation plan](backend-entrypoints.md),
[source lifecycle](source-lifecycle.md), [runtime boundaries](runtime-contract.md) and
[release/schema transitions](production-readiness-plan.md).

Several conclusions in the original audit were explicitly false or conditional even at the time. Do not copy its
counts, canonical requirements, live status, ER/Rust/AI Search obligations or September deployment observations into
current work without reading current source. The selected architecture now rejects many mechanisms the old canonical
programme required.

The full audited evidence, methodology, disputed findings and dated measurements remain in
[Git history](https://github.com/UnknownAlienHuman/eliot-research/blob/79a38b379eb7410413a2f9f615a9e681228cafd9/docs/implementation/audit-2026-09-14.md).

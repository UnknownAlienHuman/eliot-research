# eliotr-pwa

Owner-facing progressive web app. Three panels: projects and sources, investigation, evidence.

Screens: Projects, Sources, Corpus Lens, Research Chat, Investigations, Hypotheses, Compare, Audit,
Research Wiki, Reports, Evidence Viewer, Jobs, Models and Budget, System Health, Draft Inbox.

The PWA holds no authority. Every action resolves through the owner API, and health is always visible
even when a connector or provider is degraded.

Official current pages inspected in the same real browser:

- `https://m3.material.io/styles/color/roles`: pair accent and on-accent roles; use tonal surface containers,
  outline for controls and outline-variant for quiet boundaries. Primary expresses emphasis, not truth.
- `https://m3.material.io/styles/typography/applying-type`: five roles with three sizes; readable body face for
  longer passages; expressive display/headline only for short emphasis.
- `https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/canonical-examples/overview`: list-detail and supporting-pane
  layouts adapt across compact/medium/expanded; the primary reading area dominates.
- `https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview/how-it-works`: standard schemes ease to the final value;
  expressive schemes may overshoot. Reading, evidence and status geometry use restrained standard motion.
- `https://design.google/library/design-notes-material-3-expressive-liam-spradlin` redirects to the current
  Inside M3 Expressive article: expression can be quiet and must serve usability/accessibility/purpose.
